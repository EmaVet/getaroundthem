# Get Around Them

Campaign landing page for *Get Around Them*, a community grants and sponsorships campaign by Bay & Basin Community Financial Services Limited (Community Bank Sanctuary Point & Districts) with 94.9 Power FM.

- One static page: `index.html`, with inline CSS and JS. No build step.
- The eligibility check runs in the visitor's browser and sends nothing.
- The contact forms are not connected yet.
- Hidden from search engines (`noindex`) until the campaign is approved and getaroundthem.com is live.
