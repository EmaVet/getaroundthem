Open all year round

Our grant submissions are open year round. Applications submitted in SmartyGrants are looked at once a month by our grants committee, then taken to the board meeting to be discussed and voted on.

There are a few steps in the submission and approval process, so please allow a few months for approval.

What we fund

There are two programs. They have different rules, so it's worth knowing which one fits before you start.

Community grants

Funding for a project that clearly benefits the community.

  • For incorporated community and not-for-profit groups
  • Not incorporated? You can apply with an incorporated not-for-profit partner who signs the agreement and helps deliver the project
  • Your group needs an Australian bank account (any bank)
  • The project happens in Sanctuary Point and surrounding districts
Check if a grant fits

Sponsorships

Support for an event or activity, where your group gives us something in return.

  • Your group gives something back: promotion, signage, tickets or exposure
  • Open to Australian organisations and citizens with an Australian bank account
  • You must be a Bendigo Bank customer, or willing to become one
  • No other bank, broker or insurer as a sponsor
  • Apply at least eight weeks before your event
Check if a sponsorship fits

Before you start

  • We don't fund fundraising, individuals or charity donations.
  • A donation isn't a sponsorship. Sponsorship is a partnership, where your group gives us something in return.
  • Advertising isn't a sponsorship. We can't buy ads through the sponsorship program.
What neither program can fund

We can't fund or sponsor anything that:

  • is illegal, or breaks or tries to change the law
  • is fundraising, or for an individual or a charity donation
  • is a political donation
  • pays for costs you've already incurred
  • involves gambling
  • excludes or offends any part of the community
  • encourages violence or involves the use of weapons
  • mistreats, exploits or harms animals
  • creates an environmental hazard
  • puts public health or safety at risk
  • contributes to modern slavery
  • takes place outside Sanctuary Point and surrounding districts, or only outside Australia

Grants also can't go to commercial projects or anything that gives a private benefit to a person or business. The full rules are in the grant guidelines and the sponsorship guidelines.

Privacy

Privacy statement

If you fill in a form, Bay & Basin Community Financial Services Limited receives your name, your group’s name, your email or phone number, and your answers. They’re emailed to our team so we can contact you about your grant or sponsorship submission.

We don’t use them for anything else or share them outside the company. To have your details removed, email us.

The eligibility check itself runs on your device and sends nothing.
